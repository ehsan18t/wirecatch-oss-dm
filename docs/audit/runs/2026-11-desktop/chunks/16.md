## Findings

### 16-1 [P2] A file name holding `%(`, `%%`, `$NAME` or `%NAME%` sends yt-dlp's download to another folder or stops it

- Where: `crates/media/src/ytdlp/download.rs`, `download_args`
- Category: bug
- Failure: the work folder is `<output>.wcmedia`, so its path holds the file name, which comes from the site's title (`wirecatch_media::file_name`) or from what the person typed, and `sanitize` keeps `%`, `$`, `(` and `)`. `download_args` passes `work.join("media.%(ext)s")` to `-o` unescaped, and yt-dlp reads the whole string as an output template and expands environment variables in it. Whenever yt-dlp does the download itself (a format with `Transport::YtDlp`, such as DASH segments), a title `a%(ext)s` makes yt-dlp write to `amp4.mp4.wcmedia\media.mp4`. A title `$USERNAME` or `%USERNAME%` is replaced by the user's name, `%TEMP%` becomes `C#\Users\...\Temp.mp4.wcmedia\media.mp4` under the download folder, and `50%%` becomes `50%`. `process::run` starts yt-dlp in the real `work`; `printed_output` and `largest_result` look only in the real `work`, so the whole download ends as `NothingSaved` and leaves a full video and a stray folder that nothing removes. Try again does the same. A title such as `grow (5%(approx))` makes yt-dlp refuse at once with "unsupported format character".
- Evidence: `download_args` pushes the joined path as it is, with no doubling of `%`; `crates/engine/src/naming.rs` `clean` replaces only `<>:"|?*`, control and format characters. Run with the installed yt-dlp 2026.10.26 (`--simulate --print filename -o "<dir>/<name>/media.%(ext)s"`, on a local `file:` URL): `a%(ext)s.mp4.wcmedia` gave `amp4.mp4.wcmedia\media.mp4`; `%TEMP%.mp4.wcmedia` gave `C#\Users\Ehsan\AppData\Local\Temp.mp4.wcmedia\media.mp4`; `50%% off.mp4.wcmedia` gave `50% off.mp4.wcmedia\media.mp4`; `$USERNAME.mp4.wcmedia` and `%USERNAME%.mp4.wcmedia` gave `Ehsan.mp4.wcmedia\media.mp4`; `grow (5%(approx)).mp4.wcmedia` was an error. A plain `100% sure` is unchanged. No test uses such a name. A site cannot climb out of the download folder this way, since `sanitize` leaves no path separator in the name. Running `-o` with a relative template (`media.%(ext)s`, or `-P` for the folder) removes the whole class, as `process::run` already starts in `work`.
- Confidence: certain

### 16-2 [P2] A video's title is cut at its last slash when it becomes the file name

- Where: `crates/media/src/lib.rs`, `file_name`
- Category: bug
- Failure: `file_name` hands the title to `wirecatch_engine::naming::sanitize`, which treats the text as a path a server sent and keeps only what follows the last `/` or `\`. "AC/DC - Thunderstruck" is saved as `DC - Thunderstruck.mp4`; "Episode 1/10" and "Episode 2/10" are both `10.mp4` (the second then becomes a numbered copy or asks); "12/25/2020 Holiday special" is `2020 Holiday special.mp4`; a title ending in a slash is `download.mp4`.
- Evidence: `crates/engine/src/naming.rs` `clean` starts with `name.rsplit(['/', '\\']).next()`. The callers (`crates/core/src/manager/media.rs` `file_name(&choice.title, ..)`, `captures.rs`) pass yt-dlp's `info.title` with no replacement of slashes. `titles_become_safe_file_names` has no title with a slash. The fix belongs here: replace the separators in the title before `sanitize`, whose path handling is right for a server's name.
- Confidence: certain

### 16-3 [P2] A video and audio downloaded by yt-dlp show progress going backwards

- Where: `crates/media/src/lib.rs`, `MediaProgress::set_own`; `crates/media/src/ytdlp/download.rs`, `download_once`
- Category: wrong implementation
- Failure: when yt-dlp downloads a pair (`core` passes the selection `A+B` with `merge_into`), its `progress.downloaded_bytes` and totals are those of the format being downloaded. `set_own` replaces the last report, so a 1 GB video with 100 MB of audio climbs to 1 GB of 1 GB, then drops to 0 of 100 MB and ends at 100 MB of 100 MB. The person sees a finished bar start over, with a size far below the real one.
- Evidence: `State::own` holds one `(done, total)`, replaced whole on each report; `download_once` passes each report on with nothing telling the formats apart (`PROGRESS_FIELDS` has no format field); `read` uses it as the whole total when there are no parts. No test covers two formats. yt-dlp's per-format report is from its documented progress hook, not run here.
- Confidence: likely

### 16-4 [P2] A quality says "60 fps" although the format it downloads may be 30 fps

- Where: `crates/media/src/choice.rs`, `video_offer`
- Category: wrong implementation
- Failure: a 1080p row with H.264 at 30 fps in MP4 and VP9 at 60 fps in WebM is labelled "1080p, 60 fps, MP4" and its `quality` is "1080p, 60 fps", but it downloads the MP4, since MP4 video-only formats are preferred regardless of frame rate. The person gets 30 fps from a row that promised 60, and is never offered the 60 fps one under Original at that height.
- Evidence: `fps` is the largest of every format in `at`, not of the `video` chosen below it; `video_only` is `best_by_rate` of the MP4 formats first. `video_variants` does match the row's frame rate, but from the chosen video's own fps, so it does not correct this.
- Confidence: certain

### 16-5 [P3] Sizes and bit rates a page chooses are added without a check

- Where: `crates/media/src/choice.rs`, `video_offer` and `estimate`; `crates/media/src/output/variants.rs`, `variant`; `crates/media/src/lib.rs`, `MediaProgress::read`
- Category: wrong implementation
- Failure: a format with `filesize` near `u64::MAX` (read whole by `whole_number`), or an HLS `BANDWIDTH` near it, overflows `x + y`, `v + a`, `a + b` or the `sum()` in `estimate`. The release profile has no `overflow-checks`, so the shown size wraps to a small wrong number; dev and `cargo ui` builds panic.
- Evidence: the four unchecked additions in those functions; `[profile.release]` in the root `Cargo.toml` sets `panic = "abort"` and no overflow checks. docs/ENGINEERING.md section 4 requires checked or saturating arithmetic on untrusted numbers. `a_size_is_estimated_only_when_it_is_a_count` tries a huge duration but no huge bandwidth; `an_estimated_size_that_is_not_a_count_is_unknown` covers `filesize_approx` but not this sum.
- Confidence: certain
